## A little tour of the menu

From the perfect Pornstar Martini to a classic Negroni, a refreshing Mojito, a creamy Piña Colada… and everything in between - we've got something for every taste 🍹

Here's what tends to fly off the bar:

- **Pornstar Martini** - passionfruit, vanilla, and a shot of prosecco on the side. The reason a lot of people are here.
- **Negroni** - equal parts gin, Campari, sweet vermouth. No fuss, no substitutes, just a proper one.
- **Mojito** - fresh mint, lime, and rum over crushed ice. A summer staple all year round in La Marina.
- **Piña Colada** - creamy, tropical, and somehow always the right choice.

And plenty more where that came from. The menu gets a refresh from time to time, so if you've had a favourite before, come and see what's new alongside it.
