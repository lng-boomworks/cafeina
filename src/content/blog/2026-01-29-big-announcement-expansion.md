✨ **BIG ANNOUNCEMENT** ✨

As we come up to almost two years open, we've been feeling incredibly grateful. What started as a dream has become a community - and that is entirely because of you. Every coffee, every cocktail, every conversation, every celebration, every quiet moment spent here, we are endlessly thankful for the support you've shown us 🤍

## The worst-kept secret

It's probably been the worst kept secret for a while now… but we can finally share it properly:

👉 **We're going to be taking over the unit next door.**

## A thank you to our neighbour

We're so sad to be losing our lovely neighbour Michelle, and they will be genuinely missed. At the same time, we're incredibly excited for what this next chapter means and what we can build going forward.

## What's changing (and what isn't)

There will be some changes coming, but rest assured - everything you love is here to stay. Same atmosphere, same favourites, same heart. Just **more space, more opportunities**, and (hopefully) no more turning people away… well, there still might be - so you'll have to be quick! 😉

## The best is still to come

Thank you for being part of this journey with us. We wouldn't be here without you.

The best is still to come ✨🥂
