🐣🐰 **Easter Afternoon Tea at Cafeina** 🐰🐣

Celebrate the season of spring, sweetness, and togetherness with our special Easter Afternoon Tea at Cafeina Coffee & Cocktails.

## The details

- 📅 **Available:** Friday 3rd April – Friday 10th April
- 📍 **Where:** Cafeina — Luis de Góngora, 43, Consum Square, La Marina
- ⏳ **Pre-order required** — selections must be made at least 48 hours in advance

## What's on the stand

Each guest will enjoy their own beautifully presented afternoon tea stand featuring a handpicked selection:

- 🥪 Choose 3 sandwiches
- 🥧 Choose 3 savoury bites
- 🍰 Choose 3 sweet treats

From chocolatey delights to seasonal favourites, our Easter menu is bursting with flavour and festive cheer.

## Pricing

- 💸 **€16 pp** — includes unlimited tea & coffee
- 💸 **€18 pp** — includes a glass of cava
- 💸 **€21 pp** — includes a cocktail of your choice

## A note on availability

Please note the bookings for this event are very limited, and we will only be accepting a certain amount of bookings due to staff holidays. If Easter Afternoon Tea is on your list, don't leave it to the last minute.
