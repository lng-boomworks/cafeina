We've had lots of people asking when we're closing, and we've heard a few rumours about us shutting on a specific date…

Just to clear things up - **we don't have an official closing date yet** 🤍

## What we can tell you

While we officially take over the unit from the beginning of April, we're still finalising building paperwork (and as everyone knows, in Spain things are rarely as straightforward as we'd like! 🫣).

## Why we're not rushing

Trust us - we'd LOVE to be in there with our hammers already 🔨, but we want to make sure everything is done properly so we can create the best space possible for you all.

If we cut corners on the permissions and paperwork now, it'll cost us time later - and it'd mean opening a space that doesn't quite live up to what we're aiming for. Neither of those is a trade we're willing to make.

## Good things take time

Good things take time… and we promise it'll be worth the wait ✨

As always, we'll keep you updated every step of the way - thank you so much for all the love and support 🤍
