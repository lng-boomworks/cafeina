Wow… what a way to welcome the New Year! 🥂✨

Thank you so much to everyone who joined us last night — the night was overflowing with cocktails, confetti cannons, sparklers, delicious canapés, grazing boards, a red carpet moment, and truly unbelievable company. And honestly… everyone looked incredible ❤️✨

## An apology and a thank you

We'd also like to say a huge sorry to anyone we had to turn away — we were absolutely at capacity and so grateful for your understanding. It's never how we want to start the night, and it's a big part of why we're excited about what's coming next for Cafeina. Your support means the world to us.

## A short break

A little friendly reminder that we're now closed until the 7th of January for a short break — and yes, many of you will be pleased to know we did make the very early wake-up ✈️🤣. We'll be back refreshed and ready to go.

Thank you again for celebrating with us — we can't wait to welcome you all back very soon.

Cheers to 2026! 🥂✨
